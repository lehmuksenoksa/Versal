# 2. The run

What a run goes through, top to bottom: the reason to go down, the regions and their bosses, and
what keeps a run moving.

Built on round 1:
- stealth in the dark;
- about 10 levels in 3 or 4 regions, a boss closing each;
- a thin story thread;
- one saved run.

## Decided (rounds 2 and 3)

- **What keeps a run moving: the lamp's oil, and dread** (round 3). Light and dark make a loop:
  - **Light** costs oil and draws eyes. The lamp burns oil while lit; oil is found on levels; out of
    oil, you're in the dark until you find more. An oil gauge in the ledger.
  - **The dark** costs dread and hides what's there (4.2). Dread rises in the dark and falls in
    light. When it's high:
    - you hear things that aren't there (false noise words, not told from real ones);
    - your hands shake (worse blows);
    - at the worst, you panic for a turn.

    Fervor resists it.

  Resting is no longer free: resting lit burns oil, resting dark builds dread. No level-wide
  clock, no hunger.
- **Bosses guard the way down.** A boss can be killed or got past (sneaked by, lured away). Killing
  it gives more (its hoard, a level up). Each boss's lair is laid out with routes past it (part 6).

## The world's shape (round 12)

Your idea, round 12. It replaces round 8's regions and areas, which are kept in
`round8-regions-areas.md` as history.

- **Regions:** big, several maps of varying size, linked up, down and sideways.
  - The Dungeon Underneath: the dungeon, the collapsed rooms.
  - Goblin Territory: the king's halls, the warrens, the pits.
  - Abandoned Dwarven Keep: the hold, the forge.
  - The Deep Dark: the caverns, the glowing deep, the webs, the sulphur vents, the rift, the bone
    field, the underwood.
  - The Drowned City.
- **Transitions** between regions: the mines, the sewers, a breach.
- **Themed areas:**
  - the bandits' hold;
  - the archive (in the Dungeon, the Dwarven Keep, the Drowned City);
  - the cult's sanctum (in the Dungeon, the Drowned City).
- **The regions branch:** at some points you choose which comes next, and a run goes through some
  of them by its route.

## The world by depth (round 13)

- **A generic world, generated by depth**, made of today's kinds of ground carried deeper: built,
  collapsed, caves, and something deeper still. Danger follows depth.
- **The regions are set in it** and stand out against it, as are the transitions and themed
  areas. A region either replaces the world's maps at its depth (you pass through it) or branches
  off it (entered and left by a side way or a stair), region by region.
- **About half a run is the generic world.** It still has surprises, strangers, themed areas and
  monsters by depth.
- **A run is 2 to 4 hours** (changed from round 1's 1 to 2).

## Themes (round 15)

A theme bundles foes, strangers and places that belong together. A region mixes a few, and a
themed area is one. Not everything has a theme. The themes:
- the dead;
- the old faith;
- the cult (set against the old faith);
- pests;
- the garrison;
- goblins;
- the dwarves;
- the deep;
- beasts of the dark;
- the drowned;
- outlaws.

Maybe: delvers. Lists in `round15-themes.md` and the catalogue.

**The castle-like dungeon** (the garrison's rooms) is held by one holder drawn each run: the dead
garrison, the cult, or outlaws.

**The Dwarven Keep** is held by one drawn each run (round 30): the dwarven dead, orcs who overran
it, or both at war. Elementals and the dragon are there whoever holds it; golems and animated
armour only with the dead. The dragon is at war with all: holders avoid its side of the Keep
(round 34).

**Goblins:** halls and the throne room rare; camps, guard posts, warrens, pits and crude prisons
common.

## Bosses (round 19)

- The Dungeon Underneath: the lich, leading the cult or the undead, whichever holds it. When
  outlaws hold it, a stronger bandit chief instead (round 25).
- Goblin Territory: the goblin king.
- The Abandoned Dwarven Keep: the dragon.
- The Deep Dark: the dark elf prince. When the Deep Dark comes before the end game, the dark elf
  wizard made stronger instead; the prince is kept for the end game (round 28).
- The Drowned City: an eldritch horror.
