# 2. The run

What a run goes through, top to bottom: the reason to go down, the regions and their bosses, and
what keeps a run moving.

Built on round 1:
- stealth in the dark;
- about 10 levels in 3 or 4 regions, a boss closing each;
- a thin story thread;
- one saved run.

## Decided (round 2)

- **Bosses guard the way down.** A boss can be killed or got past (sneaked by, lured away). Killing
  it gives more (its hoard, a level up). Each boss's lair is laid out with routes past it (part 6).

## Open

- **The reason to go down, and the bottom** (2.1): "We can come up with something later." The
  ideas so far, kept for then: the cult waking something under the king's halls; a relic to fetch
  and bring back up; the goblin king as the end.
- **The regions** (2.2): to be decided after brainstorming regions in a round of their own.
  Round 2 sketched four, kept as a starting point:
  - the dungeon;
  - the caverns;
  - the king's halls;
  - a new region at the bottom.
- **What keeps a run moving** (2.4): "At the moment, I'm not sure what's the benefit of having a
  light on. It attracts monsters. We need to think this more." Round 3 takes it up, together with
  what light is worth (4.2).

## 2.4 What keeps a run moving (round 3)

Revisited with what light is worth (4.2, round 3). Without pressure, a careful player rests to
full after each fight and waits out anything, and the tension goes. More than one can be picked.

| | Option | Costs and consequences |
|---|---|---|
| A | **The lamp's oil** *(my pick)* | The lamp burns oil while lit; oil is found on levels; out of oil, you're in the dark until you find more. It only works if light is worth having (4.2): with nothing to see by it, there's nothing to spend. An oil gauge in the ledger. **M** |
| B | **The dark wears on you** *(my pick)* | **Dread** rises while you're in the dark and falls in light. High dread: you hear things that aren't there (false noise words on the map, which you can't tell from real ones), your hands shake (worse blows), and at the worst you panic for a turn. Fervor resists it (3.4). **M** |
| C | **The level wakes up over time** | The longer you stay, the more the monsters wander, wake and patrol. Needs a level-wide alert, read by the monsters' minds. **M** |
| D | **Hunger** | Food to find and eat. Nothing to do with light or sneaking. (Food wasn't picked in 4.5.) **M** |
| E | **None** | As now. |

**Why A and B:** together they make light and dark a loop, which is the heart of the game:
- light costs oil and draws monsters;
- the dark costs dread and hides what's there (4.2).

Neither is safe to stay in, so the player keeps choosing. Resting is no longer free either:
resting lit burns oil, resting dark builds dread. That does most of what C would, so C isn't
needed, though it could be added.
