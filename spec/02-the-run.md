# 2. The run

What a run goes through, top to bottom: the reason to go down, the regions and their bosses, and
what keeps a run moving.

Built on round 1:
- stealth in the dark;
- about 10 levels in 3 or 4 regions, a boss closing each;
- a thin story thread;
- one saved run.

## Decided (rounds 2 and 3)

- **What keeps a run moving: the lamp's oil, and dread** (round 3). Light and dark make a loop:
  - **Light** costs oil and draws eyes. The lamp burns oil while lit; oil is found on levels; out of
    oil, you're in the dark until you find more. An oil gauge in the ledger.
  - **The dark** costs dread and hides what's there (4.2). Dread rises in the dark and falls in
    light. When it's high:
    - you hear things that aren't there (false noise words, not told from real ones);
    - your hands shake (worse blows);
    - at the worst, you panic for a turn.

    Fervor resists it.

  Resting is no longer free: resting lit burns oil, resting dark builds dread. No level-wide
  clock, no hunger.
- **Bosses guard the way down.** A boss can be killed or got past (sneaked by, lured away). Killing
  it gives more (its hoard, a level up). Each boss's lair is laid out with routes past it (part 6).

## Principles for the map (round 5)

In your words: "I want the map to be random generated, but with regions with different feel and a
bit different gameplay as well. And there should be surprises on each run. Positive ones as well."

## Regions and areas (round 8)

- **Regions** (whole levels): the dungeon, the drowned city, the dwarven hold, the bandits' hold,
  the cult's sanctum.
- **Either a region or an area:** the collapsed rooms, the caverns, the sewers, the mines, the
  archive.
- **Areas** (a part of a level): the goblin king's halls, the glowing deep, the goblin warrens,
  the webs, the forge, the sulphur vents, the rift, the bone field, the underwood, the pits.
- **Each run draws its regions:** each stage of the run takes one region from the few that fit
  its depth, so runs differ while danger rises top to bottom.
