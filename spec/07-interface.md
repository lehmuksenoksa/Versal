# 7. Interface

How the player sees and controls the game. The look is the user's to decide (part 8, the visual
language); this part lists what's needed. Open, apart from what's written here.

## Slice 1: what the new mechanics need (agreed in chat)

The proof of concept's ledger already has the style, the log, the tips and click to walk. Slice 1
adds:

| Need | How |
|---|---|
| **The player's light** on or off | One key, on whichever light is in use (the torch if one is held, else the light slot; `light-and-dread.md`, section 9) |
| **The player's pace** | A key cycles sneak, walk, run; click to walk moves at the current pace; shown as a word in the ledger |
| **Equipment** | The slots shown in the pack: main hand, off hand, light, body, head, two rings (`fighting.md`) |
| **Fuel** | A gauge in the ledger for the light in use: oil for the lamp, burn for a torch or candle |
| **Dread** | A word in the ledger only: steady, uneasy, afraid, terrified (no gauge) |
| **A monster's state** | In its tip; some states also by animation (`stealth.md`) |
| **Traps** | Drawn when seen, with a tip; looking different from decoration (`features.md`) |
| **Level up** | A page: three perks to choose from, then +1 to an attribute; slice 1's one new full page |
| **Item names** | Built from tags: "a rusty goblin-made sword" |
| **Throwing, shooting, wands** | Aim at a square: a click, or keys |

Not in slice 1: the phone layout; the title, death and victory pages (`11-getting-there.md`).
