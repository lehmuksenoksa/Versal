# Round 16: the roles

You asked to open the roles up and go through them in a round of their own. A role is what a
monster does to you, seen from a sneaking player's side: how it finds you, and what kind of
trouble it is once it has. Each monster gets one (round 17), with its answer: what gets you past
it. The roles are what the cull tests: two monsters with the same role and the same answer are
one too many.

## The roles

| Role | What it does | How it plays | For example | My pick |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wanderer** | Roams the level with no post or route | The unplanned meeting: it can turn up anywhere, so no place is safe for long | Ghost | Keep |
| **Pack** | Several together, round a leader or a fire | Hard to fight, easy to see coming: their camp is a fixed obstacle to sneak round or lure away | Goblins | Keep |
| **Swarm** | Many weak ones acting as one | Dangerous in numbers, not one by one; held off by something (light, staying out of the water) rather than fought | Rats, leeches | Keep |
| **Guard** | Keeps one post and watches it | A fixed puzzle: find its blind side, distract it, or go another way | Golem, archer | Keep |
| **Patroller** | Walks a set round | A puzzle of timing: learn the round, slip through behind it | Skeleton, cultist | Keep |
| **Ambusher** | Waits still or hidden until you're close | Danger you don't see coming; the answer is noticing it first (light, tracks, searching) | Spider, mimic | Keep |
| **Tracker** | Follows the trail you leave | Hiding isn't enough, because it follows where you've been; you have to break the trail | Wolf | Keep |
| **Hunter** | Seeks you out on its own, alone | Pressure: it comes looking, so you can't wait it out; each has one thing that drives it off | Cave cat, wraith | Keep |
| **Alarm-raiser** | Not dangerous itself, but calls others | Getting seen by it is what costs you: it brings the danger to you | Shrieker, bat | Keep |
| **Brute** | Big, slow and hits very hard | Never fight it face to face; it's easy to outpace or avoid, and deadly if it corners you | Troll, ogre | Keep |
| **Caster** | Uses magic that changes things | It changes the rules nearby (lights fires, raises the dead), so it's the one to deal with first | Goblin shaman, cult priest | Keep |
| **Leader** | Leads a pack | Kill or avoid it and the pack falters or scatters | Goblin warrior, bandit chief | Keep |
| **Lure** | Draws you toward danger | A trap with a face: it tempts you off the safe way | Will-o'-wisp | Keep |
| **Thief** | Takes something and runs | It costs you things, not health; it avoids a fight | Thief | Keep |
| **Water hazard** | Lives in water and strikes from it | Makes water a place to think twice about, rather than a monster you meet | Eel | Merge: Merge into ambusher: it's an ambusher that lives in water; the water itself is the place's, not the monster's |
| **Boss** | Guards the way out of a region | The region's last test: get past it or kill it (round 2) | Goblin king | Keep |

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Breeder** | Makes more of its kind if left | A clock: the longer you leave it, the worse it gets; deal with it early or leave the area | Worms (your "Worms! also a classic"), a spider's nest | Keep |

## Pairs that shade into each other

- **Hunter and tracker.** Both come after you; the hunter searches, the tracker follows your
  trail. *My pick: keep both.* Their answers differ: you drive a hunter off, you break a
  tracker's trail.
- **Guard and patroller.** Both hold ground; the guard in a spot, the patroller along a route.
  *My pick: keep both.* One is a puzzle of space (its blind side), the other of time (its
  round).

## One role each, or more?

- **A** one role per monster *(my pick)*;
- **B** a main role and at most one more.

**Why A:** the cull test (same role, same answer) stays clean, and a monster's tags already carry
the rest of what it does. A cultist is a patroller; that it carries a lantern is a tag.
