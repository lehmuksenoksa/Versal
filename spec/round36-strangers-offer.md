# Round 36: what strangers offer, shallow

The last empty field for strangers (the content model: "what they offer: trade, a tip, help, to
follow you, a choice"). Shallow first: the kinds of offer, and a few rules for all strangers.
Which stranger offers what comes next, as a grid, once the kinds are settled. Strangers have a
Level (round 28): deeper, they offer more.

What a stranger can give you. Each stranger will have one or two.

## How many can follow you

A sellsword, a dog, a freed prisoner…

| Option | What | |
|---|---|---|
| One | One follower at a time. A new one means sending the old one off. | my pick |
| Two | Two at a time. |  |
| None | Strangers never follow; they only offer things where they are. |  |

**Why one:** a follower changes stealth (they make noise, they need light) and fights for you, so each one counts; more than one turns the game toward a party game. One is also far less to build: one follower's path-finding and choices, and one more thing for monsters to notice. **Rules out:** a party of strangers.

## When you wrong a stranger

Steal from them, attack them, or break a bargain.

| Option | What | |
|---|---|---|
| They turn on you | They fight or flee, and won't deal with you again. | my pick |
| They leave | They go, taking their offer with them. |  |
| Nothing | Strangers can't be wronged. |  |

**Why:** robbing the peddler should be a real choice with a cost, not free loot. It also makes some strangers dangerous to cross (a party of adventurers, holy warriors). **Costs:** every stranger needs a fighting side, which most monsters' families already give.

## What they want in return



| Option | What | |
|---|---|---|
| Coins and finds | They take coins, gems, relics, or things you carry. |  |
| Coins, finds or a favour | As well as paying, you can do something for them: a task in return. | my pick |
| Mostly favours | Little trade; most offers are bargains and tasks. |  |

**Why:** coins are already in (round 6: trade, round 23: coins, gems, relics); favours turn a stranger into a small story without a quest system, a task shown in the ledger. **Costs:** tasks need tracking: what's asked, what's done.

## Where strangers go



| Option | What | |
|---|---|---|
| They stay | Met where they are, and that's that. |  |
| Some are met again deeper | The peddler, a rival party, a freed prisoner: the same one turns up again further down. | my pick |
| They wander | They move about the level. |  |

**Why:** meeting the same peddler or rival party again makes the run feel like a story, and pays off a rescue (the prisoner you freed turns up to help). **Costs:** remembering a few strangers across levels: little.
