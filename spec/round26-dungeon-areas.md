## Answered

All my picks taken, and **the hall** moved from maybe to here. Nothing added under "anything missing".

- **Areas:** the barracks (with the armoury), the prison, the kitchens and stores, the hall, the
  chapel, the crypts, the lord's rooms, the flooded wing, the collapsed edge, the boss's lair.
- **Guards spread through the region:** a gatehouse where it begins, guard posts at the ways
  between areas, guardrooms beside the prison and the lord's rooms, patrols between areas, and
  guards off duty in the barracks and the hall.
- **Which areas go together**, a rule for the generator:
  - next to (a door between): the chapel and the crypts; the crypts and the boss's lair; the
    kitchens and the hall;
  - near (the same level): the hall and the barracks; the barracks and the prison; the lord's
    rooms and the hall;
  - apart (far ends or different levels): the crypts and the kitchens; the chapel and the prison;
    the lord's rooms and the collapsed edge.
- **What changes with the holder:**
  - tripwires only with outlaws;
  - with outlaws, a friendly priest keeps the chapel;
  - with the cult, the chapel is defiled: no priest, no rest at its altar;
  - with the dead garrison, the chapel stands empty, its altar still holy;
  - the prisoners are the cult's for sacrifice, the outlaws' for ransom, or the dead's, long
    forgotten;
  - the boss: the lich with the dead or the cult, a stronger bandit chief with outlaws.
