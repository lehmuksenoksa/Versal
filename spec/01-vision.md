# 1. Vision (decided, round 1)

The decisions everything else hangs on, answered on the choices page. Each has what it commits
the rest of the spec to. The options that weren't chosen, and their costs, are in this file's
history (round 1's proposal).

| # | Question | Decided | What it commits us to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The heart of the game | **Stealth in the dark.** You are weak against what lives down there. The lamp, light, noise and being seen are the main tools. Fights are dangerous, often best avoided or taken by surprise. *Changed in round 18:* stealth stays a big part, the thing that sets the game apart, but not the only thing: "A lot of the gameplay should be fighting melee, shooting enemies, retreating to a better position, running away." | The monsters' minds are the biggest piece of the systems (part 4). Levels made for sneaking (part 6). Fewer, deeper monsters (part 5) |
| 2 | What it's for | **Released free, publicly** (for example on itch.io) | Playtests by strangers, onboarding, settings, accessibility, the main browsers tested (parts 7, 10, 11) |
| 3 | Length of a run | **Middle:** about 10 levels in 3 or 4 regions, a boss closing each, 1 to 2 hours. *Changed in rounds 12 and 13:* a generic world by depth with regions set in it, **2 to 4 hours** (`02-the-run.md`) | 1 or 2 regions beyond today's ground kinds (part 2). Saving (9). Drawing caches dropped for levels not shown (part 10) |
| 4 | The character | **Choices at each level up** (skills or feats); one start for everyone | A level-up page (part 7). The choices are the run's variety, so they need care and balance by simulated runs (parts 3, 10) |
| 5 | Between runs | **Nothing carries over**, but past runs are remembered: a roll of the dead (who, how far, what killed them) | Kept in the browser, per device. A page for the roll (part 7) |
| 6 | Story | **A thin thread:** a reason to go down, told in a few lines at the start and the end; notes found on levels; rooms and regions with names and a history | Writing (part 5). Notes placed by rules (part 6). A page for notes (part 7) |
| 7 | Where it's played | **Big screens only.** Phones not supported | The interface designed once, for a mouse and keys. Drawing budgets set by laptops, not phones (part 10). What becomes of today's touch controls and low-power drawing is a part 10 question |
| 8 | Sound | **None:** the noise words on the map are its sound | The words carry everything a sound would (part 4, noise). Sound can be added later without changing the design |
| 9 | Saving | **One run, saved on leaving, gone on dying** | All of a run's state designed as plain data from the start (part 10) |
| 10 | How we work | **As in `README.md`, with experiments:** small throwaway experiments allowed when a question needs measuring, reported, never put in the game | — |
| 11 | The spec's parts and rounds | **As listed** in `README.md` | — |
