# Round 17 (draft): monsters' roles and answers

Drafted for round 16, held back until the roles are settled there (`round16-roles.md`). The first step of the content model (`content-model.md`): for each monster, its **role** (what
it does to you) and its **answer** (what gets you past it). It's test 2, "does it change how you
play?", and the cull: two monsters with the same role and the same answer are one too many.

How many of each, as proposed: ambusher 7, hunter 7, alarm-raiser 4, brute 4, guard 4, swarm 3, patroller 3, leader 2, water hazard 2, caster 2, pack 1, boss 1, tracker 1, wanderer 1, thief 1, lure 1.

## My critique

- **The answers vary well.** Light is the commonest, which suits the heart of the game, but
  there's also:
  - sound and silence (golem, deep worm, bear);
  - scent (wolf, ogre);
  - timing (skeleton, armour);
  - leaders (warrior, chief);
  - fire (troll, giant spider);
  - holy ground (wight, ghost);
  - water (eel, leeches);
  - a thrown stone (goblins, bone heap).
- **Four overlaps.** I propose cutting one of each pair:
  - **Shadow** and wraith are both darkness that moves, beaten by light. Cut the shadow (you left
    it unmarked).
  - **Giant centipede** and spider are both ambushers shown by light. Cut the centipede (your maybe
    in the dungeon).
  - **Banshee** and shrieker both wake the level. Cut the banshee (unmarked); the shrieker's answer
    (the dark) is clearer.
  - **Lake thing** and eel are both dangers in deep water. Cut the lake thing, or keep it as the
    drowned's boss.
- **Hunters are many** (7, with dark elf and wight). Each needs a different answer, as above.
