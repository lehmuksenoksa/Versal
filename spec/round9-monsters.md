# Round 9: monsters, shallow

Kinds of foes, one line each, to keep, maybe or drop. Each line says what it is and what makes it
different for sneaking. Where each is found, its numbers, how it looks and bosses are later
questions.

Already in the game, so not listed: rat, bat, spider, giant spider, skeleton, zombie, goblin,
goblin warrior, goblin king, cultist, troll.

Decided (round 6): monsters of different kinds aren't friendly to each other, and you can lure
them to fight.
