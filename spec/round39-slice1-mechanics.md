# Round 39: slice 1's mechanics

Slice 1 (`11-getting-there.md`): 3 or 4 maps of the Over Dungeon, then 2 or 3 of Goblin Territory,
the goblin king at the bottom. This round: which of the decided systems come into slice 1, and the
rebuild it carries. Every system was decided in outline in rounds 2 and 3 and later; slice 1 takes
a few and specifies them in depth. The rest wait.

What the proof of concept has already: sneaking, light and lamps, awareness (unaware, suspicious,
alarmed), noise, sneak attacks, 7 kinds of item, traits, click to walk, tips.

## Height in slice 1

Ledges, pits, chasms and rope bridges are goblin features (round 32), but height and depth have never been drawn.

| Option | What | |
|---|---|---|
| Not in slice 1 | No ledges, pits, chasms or bridges yet. | my pick |
| In, as slice 1's big new look | Height and depth drawn, and the goblins' ledges, pits and bridges with it. |  |

**Why not yet:** height is a whole visual family (part 8 lists it as open) and changes movement, sight and the generator. Slice 1 already carries the rebuild. **Costs of leaving it:** Goblin Territory is flatter than planned; it comes with a later slice.
