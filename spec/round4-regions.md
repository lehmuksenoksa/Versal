# Round 4: regions, shallow

Places only, one line each, to keep, maybe or drop. Nothing else about them is decided here.
Each of these is a later question of its own, for the kept ones only:
- who lives there;
- how it bends light and dark;
- its boss;
- how it's drawn;
- what it costs to make;
- its order in the run.

The reason to go down is a separate question too, after the regions.
