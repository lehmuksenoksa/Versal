# Round 4: a board of regions and goals

A brainstorm, not a proposal. There are 18 regions and 7 reasons to go down. For each one, mark
on the choices page:
- **keep** (it sparks);
- **maybe**;
- **drop**.

Add to any of them, mix them, or write your own in the comments. The second pass takes the kept
ones and puts together 3 or 4 regions in an order, with the goal. There will be my critique, and
pictures where a look has to be seen.

What the regions have to fit (decided):
- **About 10 levels in 3 or 4 regions, a boss guarding the way down out of each.**
- **Each level changes from one kind of ground into the next** (proof of concept). So regions
  blend at their borders, and next to each other they should make sense.
- **Stealth in the dark, with the loop of light and dark:**
  - light costs oil and draws eyes;
  - the dark costs dread and hides traps;
  - some things shun light.

  So the useful question for each region is **how it bends the loop**: lit everywhere, darker
  than dark, loud or quiet, with places to hide or none.
- What levels hold: hiding places, traps, locks, levers and hidden ways, water and ledges.

**Cost** is the drawing and the generator: **exists** (in the game now), **S** (a variant of an
existing ground), **M** (new things on an existing ground), **L** (a new kind of ground). New
looks also mean rounds of mockups.

## Regions

### R1. The dungeon *(exists)*
- **Place:** built rooms and corridors: crypts, cells, barracks, shrines.
- **Who:** the dead, rats, the first cultists.
- **Light and dark:** lit rooms between dark corridors; doors to close behind you.
- **Twist:** guard posts and patrols on straight corridors: the easiest place to learn the game.
- **Boss:** the warden of the crypts.
- **Hook:** a keep or prison built over something.

### R2. The caverns *(exists)*
- **Place:** misshapen caves, rock formations, campfires.
- **Who:** spiders, bats, goblins round their fires.
- **Light and dark:** dark, broken by campfires whose shadows make lanes to sneak along.
- **Twist:** no doors: you can't shut anything behind you; sound carries.
- **Boss:** the brood mother.
- **Hook:** the natural deep under everything built.

### R3. The goblin king's halls *(exists)*
- **Place:** rough-hewn columned halls, the throne room.
- **Who:** goblins, warriors, trolls, the king and his guard.
- **Light and dark:** braziers down the aisles, the columns striping the sides with shadow.
- **Twist:** alarm horns: one goblin's shout can wake a hall.
- **Boss:** the goblin king.
- **Hook:** a kingdom under the earth.

### R4. The catacombs *(S: the dungeon's walls, new niches)*
- **Place:** narrow passages walled with bones, niches stacked with the dead.
- **Who:** the dead sleeping in their niches, rats, grave robbers.
- **Light and dark:** dark, and the dead slowed in light.
- **Twist:** bone floors crunch (loud); the niches are hiding places, but some of them hold the
  dead, who wake to noise.
- **Boss:** the bone choir: many skulls singing; silence them to pass.
- **Hook:** who buried so many, and why under here?

### R5. The barrows *(M: tombs, sarcophagi, sealed doors)*
- **Place:** the old kings' tombs: mounds, burial halls, treasure.
- **Who:** wights (who see in the dark), tomb guardians, traps of every kind.
- **Light and dark:** dark, and full of traps, so light is worth the most here.
- **Twist:** the trap region; sealed doors opened by levers; treasure that wakes the dead when
  taken.
- **Boss:** the barrow king.
- **Hook:** the kings who ruled before the goblins.

### R6. The drowned city *(M: deep water, wading, ruins)*
- **Place:** an old city half under black water; streets you wade, halls you swim.
- **Who:** the drowned dead, eels, lurkers below.
- **Light and dark:** water drowns the lamp: deep water is total dark.
- **Twist:** wading is loud, swimming silent but blind; things under the surface you can't see.
- **Boss:** the thing in the cistern.
- **Hook:** someone flooded it on purpose.

### R7. The goblin warrens *(S: caverns, cramped)*
- **Place:** cramped goblin-dug tunnels, many small dens.
- **Who:** goblins and their young, rats, wolves or worse kept as pets.
- **Light and dark:** dim fires in every den.
- **Twist:** tunnels too narrow to pass two abreast; alarm bells strung through the warren; nowhere
  far from someone.
- **Boss:** the warren mother.
- **Hook:** the king's halls' underbelly.

### R8. The glowing deep *(L: glowing fungus, its own light)*
- **Place:** caves where fungus glows: soft light everywhere, no true dark.
- **Who:** spore-folk, beetles, the dead grown over.
- **Light and dark:** never dark, never bright: dread stays low, but you're always faintly seen.
- **Twist:** puffballs burst when stepped on (noise, and a cloud: poison or sleep); cut the
  glowing caps to make dark.
- **Boss:** the mother cap.
- **Hook:** a beauty that feeds on what dies there.

### R9. The webs *(M: caverns, webs)*
- **Place:** caves strung with webs, cocoons hanging.
- **Who:** spiders of every size, their cocooned prey (some alive).
- **Light and dark:** dark; fire burns webs (and lights the cave).
- **Twist:** webs tremble when touched and the spiders feel it, so touch is the noise here; burn a
  way through, loud and bright.
- **Boss:** the brood mother (if not in the caverns).
- **Hook:** the cocoons hold those who went before you.

### R10. The mines *(L: shafts, rails, scaffolds, chasms)*
- **Place:** an abandoned mine: tunnels, rails and carts, scaffolds, shafts.
- **Who:** miners gone wrong, ghosts, crawlers from what they dug into.
- **Light and dark:** dark, with miners' lanterns left behind (oil plentiful).
- **Twist:** ledges and drops; carts to ride (fast, loud) or push into things; tunnels that fall in.
- **Boss:** the foreman, or what the miners found.
- **Hook:** they dug too deep.

### R11. The forge *(L: lava, furnaces)*
- **Place:** a forge still burning: furnaces, lava channels, anvils.
- **Who:** fire-touched goblins, salamanders, forge guardians.
- **Light and dark:** lit red everywhere by fire that can't be put out; shadows only behind things.
- **Twist:** the hammers' din covers your noise (and theirs); hot floors; shove into the lava.
- **Boss:** the forge-master.
- **Hook:** what is being forged, and for whom?

### R12. The frozen deep *(L: ice, snow)*
- **Place:** ice caves, a frozen lake, frost-bound halls.
- **Who:** the frozen dead, ice trolls, white things.
- **Light and dark:** ice throws light far (bright when lit); snow muffles.
- **Twist:** snow is quiet, ice loud and slippery (you slide); thin ice over black water.
- **Boss:** the white wyrm.
- **Hook:** something kept here cold on purpose.

### R13. The rift *(L: a chasm, bridges, ledges)*
- **Place:** a vast crack in the world crossed by bridges, ledges along its walls.
- **Who:** bats in clouds, cliff-crawlers, things that fly.
- **Light and dark:** the rift swallows light (it can't light far); wind.
- **Twist:** drops everywhere (ledges, shoving, falling a level down); bridges to cut.
- **Boss:** the bat-mother, or a thing in the depths of the rift.
- **Hook:** what split the world?

### R14. The archive *(M: built rooms, shelves, reading rooms)*
- **Place:** the sages' library: stacks, reading rooms, scroll cases.
- **Who:** animated books, wardens, the archivist's servants.
- **Light and dark:** candles by the hundred, and fire a danger to everything.
- **Twist:** the quiet place: every noise carries far; many notes and scrolls (Acumen's region);
  reading needs light.
- **Boss:** the archivist (long dead, still reading).
- **Hook:** the knowledge of what sleeps below.

### R15. The underwood *(L: roots, pale forest)*
- **Place:** giant roots grown down through the rock: a pale forest underground.
- **Who:** burrowers, root-things, blind hunters.
- **Light and dark:** dark; the hunters are blind and hunt by sound alone, so light doesn't matter
  to them.
- **Twist:** roots as cover everywhere (hiding places); the ground carries your steps to those
  below.
- **Boss:** the root-heart.
- **Hook:** what grows above, so deep its roots reach here?

### R16. The bandits' hold *(S: dungeon, near the surface)*
- **Place:** a hideout dug under a ruin: storerooms, a mess hall, cells.
- **Who:** bandits (people: they need light, carry lanterns, shoot crossbows), their dogs.
- **Light and dark:** lantern patrols: you see them coming, they see what their light falls on.
- **Twist:** a first region alternative: people as foes, shooting, alarms, locked doors and keys.
- **Boss:** the bandit chief.
- **Hook:** they found the way down and sell what comes up.

### R17. The cult's sanctum *(M: carved temple, pools, ritual circles)*
- **Place:** a carved temple: pools, ritual circles, cells, an altar.
- **Who:** cultists, the dead they raise, the high priest.
- **Light and dark:** candles everywhere: put them out to make your dark, and the cultists come to
  relight them.
- **Twist:** the light war: the region most about dousing (round 2's light).
- **Boss:** the high priest.
- **Hook:** the cult thread (G1).

### R18. The prison of what sleeps *(L: the strange place)*
- **Place:** where the sleeping thing lies: a place that doesn't hold to the rules: rooms that
  shift, walls that weren't there.
- **Who:** what the thing dreams, its keepers.
- **Light and dark:** the dark spreads and your light shrinks; dread at its worst.
- **Twist:** the last test of the loop: oil scarce, dread rising, nothing to rest on.
- **Boss:** what sleeps, or keeping it asleep.
- **Hook:** the end of the cult thread, or of any other.

## Reasons to go down

### G1. The cult and what it wakes
The cultists serve something sleeping below, and are waking it; you go down to stop them. A
thread through every region (their notes, their marks, their patrols). Ends in the sanctum or the
prison.

### G2. A relic, and the climb back
Something at the bottom to fetch. Taking it wakes the levels above, and you climb out through
them, changed. Levels used twice, but kept and changed: more saving, its own rules.

### G3. Someone lost
Someone of yours went down before you. You follow their notes level by level (the notes the
spec already has), and at the bottom find what became of them.

### G4. Out, not down
You're thrown into the oubliette at the very bottom, with nothing. The run climbs up and out. You
start weakest where it's deepest: a stealth start by force. The order of regions is turned over,
and the surface is the end.

### G5. The last light
The light is going out in the world above, and its source (a flame, a stone) is down here, stolen
or failing. Light as the story, not only the rules: oil and dread at the heart of the end.

### G6. A curse
You carry a curse, and the cure is at the bottom. Dread could be its mark: it grows as you go
deeper, and the end lifts it.

### G7. The goblin king's bounty
As now: a price on the king's head. Simple, and a goblin is a weak final threat for ten levels.
