# Features: the rules for slice 1's

From round 43 (yours, quoted there) and the trap rules agreed in chat after it. Numbers marked (test).

## Water

- **Shallow water:** wading, slow and loud (as round 22).
- **Deep water:** swimming: silent, but the light the player holds goes out (round 22). Shoving a foe
  into it (`fighting.md`).
- **A current, in shallow or deep water, moves the player or a monster** along it each turn (yours:
  "A current moves player or monster").

## Doors and ways

- **Doors and secret doors,** as in the proof of concept (secret doors found by searching).
- **A barricade** "can be attacked to be opened. Takes X damage to break. Makes noise. Cannot be
  closed again." (X: test.)
- **A gate** is "seen through" (it doesn't block sight or light); "a lever somewhere on the same map
  closes or opens" it.
- **No narrow way as a feature:** a corridor or tunnel one square wide does it, by layout.

## Traps (agreed in chat)

- **Found in light:** a trap in a lit or dim square within the player's sight is seen (round 3:
  traps show only in light). In the dark it's found by walking into it, or by searching (a turn), as
  secret doors are.
- **What each does:**
  - **tripwire:** rings a bell, a loud sound that rouses the side that set it, as a gong
    (`stealth.md`, section 8);
  - **pressure plate:** darts, for damage;
  - **net:** "Holds you while monsters come. Can be attacked. Takes X damage to break." (X: test.)
- **Monsters and traps:** monsters know their own side's traps and step round them; others walk into
  them. So a trap can be turned on monsters (round 2): a goblin tripwire catches a cultist led there.
- **Spent once sprung:** a wire rung, darts shot, a net cut.
- **Who sets them:** goblins and bandits; traps come with encounter templates (a tripwire at a camp's
  approach, a net in a guarded corridor).

## Containers

- **Chests:** not locked in slice 1.
- **Crates and barrels** "can be broken. Some contain things" (breaking: a turn and a noise, test).
- **Nests and hoards:** goblin stashes, rat nests, spider webs.
- **A look rule:** "Containers to interact with need to be graphically different from decorations."
