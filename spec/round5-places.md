# Round 5: smaller places, shallow

"Not everything needs to be a full level though. Can we brainstorm more, maybe smaller areas,
even just special rooms."

Places only, one line each, to keep, maybe or drop, as in round 4. Two sizes:
- **Areas:** a part of a level (a few rooms, a stretch of cave).
- **Rooms:** a single room.

Left for later, each its own question:
- where each belongs (which regions, or anywhere);
- what's in it;
- who's there;
- how it's drawn.

So is whether some of round 4's regions would be better as areas.

Already in the game, so not listed:
- the rooms: storeroom, gallery, pillared hall, crypt, jail, barracks, dining hall, shrine, the
  throne room;
- the goblins' campfire camp in the caves;
- flooded and infested rooms.
