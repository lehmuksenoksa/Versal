aren't asked here.

## My critique

- **It's the first region, and it teaches the game.** It should have few kinds of monster, each
  easy to learn: the game's own (rat, spider, skeleton, zombie, cultist) and a few more.
  Strength-draining wights and wall-walking ghosts are harsh this early.
- **Some things belong to other places.** Kobolds and lost miners belong to the mines; the
  flooded wing to the Drowned City; the library to the archive; a cult priest to the sanctum.
  The themed areas can bring theirs into the dungeon when they're found there.
- **It has many rooms, and that's right.** It's a built place, and its rooms are its variety.
  Culling there is lighter.
- **A separate question first:** how many kinds of monster a region should have. It applies to
  every region, so it's asked once.
