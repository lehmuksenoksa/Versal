# Round 6: what the places allow, shallow

Three lists drawn from the places (`places.md`) and your principles (random maps, regions that
feel and play differently, surprises every run, good ones too). Each line is a separate keep,
maybe or drop. How any of them works is a later question.

## Features the places call for

| # | Feature | In a line | From |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Other ways between levels | Sinkholes, wells, shafts, lifts, stair towers: falls one way, climbs the other | Sinkhole, dry well, winch room, stair tower |
| 2 | Digging | A pickaxe to dig new tunnels and reach sealed places (your idea) | Sealed wing, mine-head |
| 3 | Friendly characters | People to meet who aren't foes | Hermit, prisoners, market (your idea) |
| 4 | Trade | Buying and selling with someone | Market (your idea) |
| 5 | Freeing prisoners | Prisoners who help, guide or follow once freed | Prison block, torture chamber |
| 6 | Maps | A map room or a found map fills in your map of the level (your idea) | Map room |
| 7 | Beasts let loose | Monsters set on other monsters | Beast pens |
| 8 | Mending and bettering gear | At a smithy or workshop | Smithy, workshop |
| 9 | Brewing | Making or finding out potions at an alchemist's | Alchemist's room |
| 10 | Places to rest | Somewhere rest is safer or better | Hot spring, bath, chapel |
| 11 | Gates | A portcullis or door to drop between you and them | Gatehouse |
| 12 | A fight for a prize | An arena fight you can choose to take | Fighting pit |
| 13 | Choices with a cost | Spare or not, save or not, take or leave | Nursery, sacrifice room |
| 14 | Light that carries | Crystal or water throwing light further | Crystal grotto, pool room |
| 15 | Guardians of special rooms | A strong foe in one room, less than a boss | Sarcophagus chamber, treasury |
| 16 | Places reached only by hidden ways | Rooms behind false walls and secret doors | Smugglers' stash, sealed wing |
| 17 | Monsters at each other | Two kinds that fight when they meet | Spider nest, goblin camp |
