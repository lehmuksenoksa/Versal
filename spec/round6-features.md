# Round 6: what the places allow, shallow

**Answered.** Most kept; the answers and comments are in the last column. Comments that set
something:
- **Friendly characters:** "A group of friendly adventurers, a dwarven expedition to a lost keep.
  We need more." (round 7)
- **Monsters:** "Monsters of different types aren't friendly. Player should be able to lure monsters
  to fight each other."
- **Gates:** "But it can't block progress."
- **Hidden ways:** "It'd be nice if they were hinted somehow. Rubble filled corridor. Marks on the
  floor going through a wall, etc."
- **Mending gear:** "Maybe a one time thing: enhance a weapon or armor."
- **Dropped:** brewing; your past runs; the thread's marks ("No real story here").

feel and play differently, surprises every run, good ones too). Each line is a separate keep,
maybe or drop. How any of them works is a later question.
