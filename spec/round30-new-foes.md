## Worms, reworked

The worm we had (Depth 8, huge, digs through rock) becomes the dangerous variety, deeper.

| Foe | What | My pick |
|---|---|---|
| Worm | Worms, common, Depth 1: small and easy alone, but breeds fast; leave one and there are many (The breeder role (round 16)) | Here |
| Great worm | Worms, greater, Depth 13: huge, digs through rock, hunts by sound; the Deep Dark's (The old worm, moved deeper) | Here |
| Worm mother | Worms, special, Depth 6: a fat, slow worm the small ones come from; kill it and they stop (An answer to the breeding) | Maybe |

**Why a mother:** a breeder needs an answer besides killing every one. If you find and kill her, the breeding stops. That's a stealth-or-fight choice: slip past the brood to reach her, or fight your way through.

## The dwarven dead: a family, or a tag?

Your dwarven dead could be a family of their own, or the dead family with a dwarven tag (round 10's tags).

| Option | What | |
|---|---|---|
| Their own family | The dwarven dead: a family with its own ranks: warrior, crossbowman, high guard, rune lord. | my pick |
| The dead, tagged dwarven | Skeletons and the rest, with a dwarven tag that changes their look and gear. |  |

**Why a family:** your names are ranks that play differently: the crossbowman shoots, the high guard holds a door, the rune lord casts. That's what a family and its ranks are for (round 18). A tag changes what a monster is like, not what it does. **Costs:** four monsters to draw and balance instead of one tag. **Rules out:** dwarven zombies, dwarven ghosts and the like for free; they'd each be one more member.

## Orcs: a family of their own?

Orcs would sit beside goblins and giants. To be worth it they must play differently from goblins.

| Option | What | |
|---|---|---|
| Yes, a family | Orcs in war bands: they fight in the open, loud, strong, carrying torches. A foe to fight or avoid, not to sneak among. | my pick |
| No | Goblins, ogres and trolls are enough. |  |

**Why:** goblins sneak and swarm; orcs would be the opposite: easy to see and hear coming, hard to beat head on. That gives the Keep a fighting foe beside its slow golems and the dead. **The risk:** sameness with goblins; it holds only if they behave differently. **Costs:** a family of about five.

## Who holds the Keep

Like the Dungeon Underneath (round 15), the Keep could be held by one of a few, drawn each run. Golems, elementals and the dragon are there whoever holds it.

| Option | What | |
|---|---|---|
| Drawn each run | The dwarven dead, or orcs who overran it. | my pick |
| Both, at war | The orcs fight the dwarven dead in every run. |  |
| No holder | Orcs are one more foe there, beside the dead. |  |

**Why drawn:** it worked for the Dungeon: the same rooms play differently run to run. The end game is often the Keep, so this varies it most. **Both at war** is fun once (lure one onto the other) but makes the Keep crowded every time. **Costs:** a holder rule for the Keep, as the Dungeon has.
