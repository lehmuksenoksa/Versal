# 3. The character

One start for everyone. The run's variety comes from choices made at each level up (round 1).

## Decided (rounds 2 and 3)

- **Levels are earned by going deeper and finding things out.** Experience for:
  - new ground seen, and each new level reached;
  - secrets found and notes read;
  - sneaking: getting past a monster that would have noticed you, and sneak attacks;
  - bosses, killed or got past.

  Kills give a little.
- **At a level up, one of three offered:** three choices drawn from a pool (about 30 to 40),
  grouped in families so the player can lean into one. Rare or strong ones come only deeper. A
  page shows the three.
- **Attributes, with the numbers worked out from them.** In your words: "Health, attack, defense,
  speed, sneakiness, mana/magic derived from attributes. I'd like them to be a bit different (more
  flavorful) than traditionally:"
  - **Grit**
  - **Brawn**
  - **Cunning**
  - **Fervor**
  - **Acumen**

- **What each attribute drives** (round 3):

  | Attribute | What it is | Drives |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Grit** | Toughness, holding on | **Health.** Resisting bleeding and poison. Blows taken (part of **defence**, with armour) |
  | **Brawn** | Strength | **Damage** of blows. Heavy weapons and armour without their penalties. Shoving. How much you carry |
  | **Cunning** | Quickness, guile | **Sneakiness**. **Hitting** (any weapon, thrown or shot). Traps and locks |
  | **Fervor** | Will, faith, zeal | **Mana** (half). Resisting dread and fear. Standing against the dead and the cult |
  | **Acumen** | Mind, learning | **Mana** (half). The power of scrolls, wands and spells. Finding out unknown things sooner, more in the tips. Noticing secrets and traps |

  **Speed** isn't worked out from an attribute. It's moved only by particular things (heavy armour,
  swiftness, a level-up choice). To check by simulated runs: that an all-Cunning character isn't
  simply best.
- **How attributes grow** (round 3): each level up, a choice of three, and +1 to an attribute of
  your own pick. Everyone starts with all five at the same middle value.
- **Magic: spells, one mana pool** (round 3), from Fervor and Acumen. Spells are learned from
  level-up choices and scrolls (reading one teaches it or casts it once, settled in part 5). Mana
  comes back slowly, faster at rest. Known spells are listed in the ledger, and each spell is drawn
  where it shows on the map. For a stealth game:
  - darkness;
  - silence;
  - douse (a light from afar);
  - sleep;
  - blink;
  - sight through walls;
  - a ward against the dead.

## Ideas for level-up choices (kept for their brainstorm)

- **Hiding as choices** (your idea, round 22): hiding in a grid, turn-based game comes from the
  light and what blocks sight, and from choices made at a level up rather than features of the
  map. Your example: the first step in light counts as darkness, to make running between shadows
  easier.

## Attributes in slice 1 (discussed in chat, after fighting)

- **Fewer to start** (yours: "Start with fewer"). Round 3's five checked against what slice 1 has:
  cunning did too much (quieter and more accurate with everything); fervor and acumen were thin
  without spells and traps. Slice 1 starts with fewer; the others come when their systems do.
- **Grit, brawn and cunning** (agreed in chat), each owning one way to play:

  | Attribute | Drives in slice 1 | The character it makes |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Grit** | Health; resisting poison; steadiness: dread rises slower (fervor's job until fervor comes) | The survivor: lasts longer, in the dark too |
  | **Brawn** | Melee damage and melee accuracy; shoving; heavy gear's costs lessened (chainmail's noise, the warhammer's slowness); carrying | The fighter: wins in the open, holds a doorway |
  | **Cunning** | Quieter steps (hush, `stealth.md`); shooting accuracy; later, traps and locks | The sneak and the archer: unseen, and deadly at range |

- **Fervor and acumen come later**, with spells, traps and the rest; then grit hands dread back to
  fervor. Perception (dark sight, hearing, secrets) has no attribute in slice 1: fixed, changed by
  perks and items.
- **Growth as round 3:** at each level up, a perk and +1 to an attribute of the player's choice.
- **First balance check (maths):** cunning pairs stealth with shooting; shooting needs the target in
  sight and the dark makes shots miss more.

## Perks (discussed in chat, after attributes)

Level-up choices are called **perks** (after round 39). Round 3: one of three at each level up,
drawn from a pool, in families; rare or strong ones deeper.
- **Slice 1:** a pool of 12 to 15, for 6 to 8 level ups.
- **What a perk may be:** it bends a rule, opens something new to do, or adds to a number (yours:
  "I think some numbers might be good too: better dark vision etc"). Proposed: numbers that no
  attribute drives (dark sight, hearing, oil, speeds) can be perks; numbers attributes drive
  (accuracy, damage, health) are left to the +1 attribute, so the two don't overlap.
- **Families follow the ways to play** (proposed): shadow (stealth), blade (melee), eye (shooting),
  endurance (grit, dread).
