# Tags

What everything is described by (round 10): monsters first, then things, rooms and strangers.
A type has its own tags. An individual can have one extra from its type's short list.

This is round 11's list with your answers and comments applied. **Open** marks what still needs
your say. Which tags are a type's own and which extras, the numbers, and how each shows are later
questions.

## What it is

- **Beast**: an animal.
- **Undead**: risen from death; never flees. Your rename of "dead". It's also the extra that
  changes what a monster is (undead rats), so it's one tag either way.
- **Spirit**: without a body.
- **People**: human folk.
- **Goblinkin**: goblins and their kin.
- **Deep thing**: born of the deep, like nothing above.
- **Fungus**: a walking or growing mushroom.
- **Made**: built, not born (armour, golems, gargoyles).

Out: vermin ("Beast+small+pack should already tell this").

## Size

- **Tiny**: hard to hit.
- **Small**: fits through gaps.
- **Large**: hits hard; can't fit everywhere.
- **Huge**: fills a passage.

**Open:** you asked "We probably also need medium size? Depends what these are used for." Size is
used for:
- fitting through gaps;
- how easily it's hit;
- blocking a passage;
- what can be shoved (a smaller thing by a bigger).

My proposal: **no size tag means medium**, the size of a person, so most monsters carry none.

## Company

- **Alone**: keeps to itself.
- **Pack**: moves and fights as a group. Calls for help when alarmed (your fold of "calls for
  help").
- **Swarm**: many as one.
- **Leader**: leads a pack; the pack falters when it falls.
- **Guard**: keeps a post.
- **Patrol**: walks a round.

Unmarked: pair.

## Senses

- **Sight**: sees as you do, by light.
- **Dark-sight**: sees in the dark.
- **Blind**: doesn't see.
- **Keen ears**: hears far.
- **Deaf**: doesn't hear.

Unmarked: scent, tremor, life-sense, watchful (in the game).

## How it moves

## Mind

- **Mindless**: follows its nature.
- **Animal**: clever as a beast.
- **Intelligent**: opens doors, uses things (picks up weapons, drinks potions), calls for help.
  Your fold of "uses things" and "calls for help".

Out: remembers. Unmarked: cunning.

## Temper

- **Peaceful**: doesn't attack unless attacked.
- **Wary**: keeps its distance; attacks when cornered.
- **Territorial**: attacks only near its lair.
- **Hostile**: attacks on sight.
- **Savage** (in the game): attacks harder, never flees.
- **Timid** (in the game): flees when hurt.
- **Relentless**: never gives up a chase.
- **Starving** (in the game): weaker, but relentless.
- **Sleepy** (in the game): often asleep, deeply.

## Attacks

- **Venomous** (in the game): poisons.
- **Bleeds**: its blows make you bleed.
- **Drains**: takes strength (an attribute).
- **Numbs**: its touch slows you.
- **Knocks back**: throws you a square.
- **Reach**: strikes from two squares.
- **Shoots**: attacks from afar.
- **Cloud**: breathes spores, smoke or gas.
- **Steals**: takes something and runs.
- **Veteran** (in the game): hits more often and harder.

Unmarked: grabs.

## Defence

- **Tough**: more health.
- **Weak**: less health. Your generalising of "crumbling".
- **Regenerates**: heals as it goes.
- **Immune to poison.**
- **Fears fire**: fire drives it off.

## Magic

- **Magic user**: casts spells.
- **Raises the dead.**
- **Heals others.**
- **Blinks**: steps a few squares at once ("Blink dogs are a classic").
- **Darkens**: makes a patch of dark.

Unmarked: curses.

## Life

- **Never sleeps.**
- **Rises again**: comes back once after falling.
- **Breeds**: more of them over time ("Worms! also a classic").

Unmarked: eats the dead.

## Deceit

- **Disguised**: looks like a thing (the mimic).
- **Stone-still**: looks like a statue (the gargoyle).
- **Unseen in the dark**: can't be seen outside light.

Out: lures.

## Extras that change what it is

- **Undead** (the same tag as above).
- **Giant**: a great one of its kind.
- **Ghostly**: without a body.
- **Rotting**: diseased; its bite sickens.
- **Feral**: gone wild, savage, without its wits.
