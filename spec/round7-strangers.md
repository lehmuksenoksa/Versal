# Round 7: more friendly strangers, shallow

"A group of friendly adventurers, a dwarven expedition to a lost keep. We need more."

More strangers, each with a reason to be down here, alone or in a small group (no societies). Keep,
maybe or drop. What each does for you (trade, help, follow, tell) is a later question.

Kept already (round 6): a fellow adventurer, a prisoner, an escaped slave, a lost miner, a hermit,
an exile, a pilgrim, a priest, a scholar, a mapmaker, a peddler, a treasure hunter, a smuggler, a
trapper, a sellsword, a kindly ghost. Maybe: a lone survivor; the last dwarf ("A friendly undead
dwarf could be a nice twist").
