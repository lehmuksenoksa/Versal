# Monsters

**The roster is yours** (round 18), organised by family and rank in the catalogue
(`data/catalogue.json`, and the catalogue page's Bestiary view).

In your words: "There's something I don't like about this approach. Too piece meal. And maybe too
focused on the stealth aspect. I think stealth should be a big part of the game, maybe the thing
that makes this game unique among roguelikes, but it shouldn't be the only thing. A lot of the
gameplay should be fighting melee, shooting enemies, retreating to a better position, running
away."

## The bosses (round 19)

Yours:
- **The Dungeon Underneath:** the lich, leading the cult or the undead, whichever holds it that
  run (round 15).
- **Goblin Territory:** the goblin king.
- **The Abandoned Dwarven Keep:** the dragon.
- **The Deep Dark:** the dark elf prince, with the dark elves.
- **The Drowned City:** an eldritch horror, with a new family of its kind: abominations, shadows,
  lurkers.

## Ranks

How a family fights. Not every family has every rank.

- **Common**: the ordinary kind.
- **Greater**: a bigger, deeper kind (giant rat, dire wolf, greater elementals, golems of bronze,
  iron and steel).
- **Warrior**: fights in melee.
- **Archer**: shoots.
- **Caster**: magic.
- **Leader**: leads the others.
- **Boss**: a region's last test.
- **Special**: works its own way (the bone heap, the ghost, the thief, the beasthound).
