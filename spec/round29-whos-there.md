# Round 29: who's there, in every region, shallow

The next layer across the regions (round 27's plan): each region's foes and strangers. The
Dungeon Underneath was settled in round 25, so it isn't asked again.

Each foe shows its family and its Depth (round 28). A foe appears in a region only from its Depth
on, so the depth range of each region is shown too. Two foes are deeper than the region they're
listed in: the troll in Goblin Territory, the abomination in the Drowned City. They're marked maybe.

Bosses and leaders placed as a region's boss come to their lair wherever it is. Below their
Depth, the Level rule (round 28) makes them weaker, so a goblin chieftain leading an outpost at
depth 4 is a lesser one.

How many of each, how often, and what each stranger offers are later questions.
