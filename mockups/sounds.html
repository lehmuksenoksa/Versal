Fight words

The same fight, on a loop, with the words as the game shows them now and as proposed. Goblins mutter and an alarm goes up; you whack (hitting and missing), they stab back, two fall; then it calms down. Proposed: while blows are flying the background noise steps back; the fighting words fly off from the action into open space, where they don't cover each other; hits pop out in the direction of the blow, misses sink, death cries jolt and sink.
