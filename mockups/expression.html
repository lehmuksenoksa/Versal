Hatching expression

How the game shows the map: one hatching for all the rock (our cells with zones, drawn as if the whole map were solid stone), inked by a depth map from the known walls; what is in sight drawn over what is remembered, and the candle over both. Click to place the player; walk with the arrows or WASD. "Copy settings" gives the values for the game's defaults.
