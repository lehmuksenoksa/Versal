Monster letters

Every letter, yours included, in Texturina as in the game: under the candle's full light (warmed), in its dim and dark bands, and on unlit floor. Close up first, then a strip of them all at game size. Each has its own outline, chosen beside its name: paper (fading with the light, as the game's halo does) or ink, both as thick. The switches try fixes for washed-out colours, letters too bright in the dark, and letters on unlit floor.
