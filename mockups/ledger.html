The ink ledger, third round

The sidebar is always there now. On top it says who you are and how you are: your name, class and level, then your health as current over most, and anything ailing you. Under that, your sheet (experience and stats), or what you've opened instead: equipment, inventory, look. The place and the newest messages stay on the map, on the hatching; m pulls the rest down. Press the keys (click the screen first) or use the buttons; the same key closes again.

The torn plates' shape

Each plate grows as a clump of squares around what it must cover, smoothed and cut as cave walls are (some squares added, some bitten away, the corners jittered and some cut off), then torn along its edge as before. Roll new shapes until one looks right, adjust it with the sliders, then copy the text below and send it to me; "Save" keeps it in a list on this page for you.

The sidebar's top

Always the same, whatever is open under it: your name, then your class and level; your health under its heading, current over most; then what ails you, each in its colour (none yet in the game: poisoned, bleeding, and so on). Low health turns red.

Your sheet

What the sidebar shows when nothing else is open: experience, gained over needed for the next level, with a hairline of how far along; then the stats as a ledger. The keys for the other screens along the bottom, the open one lit.

On the map

The place, the level ("1st level"), and the newest three messages, set straight on the hatching with a black outline, as the creatures' letters have one, instead of on a fade to black.

On a phone

No room for a sidebar beside the map. The top carries your health on the right of the place; equipment, inventory, look and your sheet open as a sheet from the bottom.
