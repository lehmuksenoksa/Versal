The ink ledger, again

The ledger from the six proposals, refined. On top of the map: the place, your health, and the newest messages, fading; pull the older ones down as a drawer. The sidebar opens only when asked: equipment, inventory, or look at what's in sight. Press the keys (click the screen first) or use the buttons; the same key closes it again.

Open:
Desktop, 1280 by 800
Phone, 390 wide

On top

The place as the heading, as you liked; health beside it, always there, so nothing else has to be open in a fight. The newest messages under it in the sounds' italic, older ones fading. A small "m for more" under them; on a phone, a tap on the lines.

The drawer

The whole log of this level pulled down over the map, newest at the bottom, grouped by turn. What happened to you in red. Closes with m, Esc, or by pushing it back up.

Equipment and inventory

Two ledgers. Equipment is what you wear and hold, by where it goes, with what each gives; your attack and defence are the sums, written under it, so the stats live where they come from. Inventory is what you carry, lettered for using (q, then a letter), with room left.

Look

Everything in sight: creatures with how aware they are, then things. With marks on, the same ! and ? appear over the letters on the map while it's open, so the list and the map point at each other.
