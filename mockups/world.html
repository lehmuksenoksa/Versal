The world plan

The world, top to bottom as you go down. First the Over Dungeon, the general dungeon where any monster can turn up, by depth. Then where the themed areas (regions, smaller regions, transitions and sub-regions) can appear, and a card for each: its monsters, its locations, and what's found only there. A draft from your outline (round 21), with your notes on 2 or 3 regions a run, their small versions, twenty levels, and at least five themed areas a run. Monster danger is counted in rats (round 20), and every number sketches the shape of the climb, to be tuned later.
