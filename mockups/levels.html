Level areas

A whole level, fully lit. It is split into regions, each a dungeon (built rooms, straight corridors), cavern (misshapen caves with rock formations, winding passages) or collapsed (both mixed, rooms half fallen in, rubble). Corridors change as they cross from one region into the next.
