Hatching mockups

Ways to fill the rock around the same sample map. The default settings imitate the hatching in Dyson Logos' map. The controls change alternatives A, B, C and E (the wall controls change all five). Strokes in a group bend the same way and start from the same end: the end that grows out of the line or wall the group begins from. D is the game's current hatching, for comparison. New layout redraws everything with a fresh random seed.
