The sidebar

Six ways to frame the map, each on the same moment from the game: you in the old crypts, hurt, two skeletons in the room. The left picture is a desktop screen, the right the same idea on a phone. They differ on purpose, to argue about; the parts can be mixed. The stats, items and messages are made up for the milestone to come.

      Ideas to mix into any of them

      • Awareness over the letters. A small mark above a monster: nothing when unaware, ? when suspicious, ! when alarmed. Readable on the map itself, so no list is needed.
      • Seen or hidden. One word or an eye that shows whether anything has noticed you. Sneaking with the lamp out becomes something you can read, not guess.
      • Items by their classic letters. ) weapons, [ armour, ! potions: the same lettering as the creatures, so items on the floor and in the pack match.
      • Hurt shown on the @. The player's outline or the screen's edge reddens below a third of health, so the sidebar isn't needed in a fight.
      • Messages by kind. What you do in light text, what's done to you in red, what you hear in italic (the sounds already float on the map).
      • Place and depth as a heading. "The old crypts" rather than "Dlvl 1". The levels each have a feel; the name says so.
      • The keys beside each action. On desktop the key, on a phone the button: the same list, so the help overlay mostly writes itself.
      • Turns or time. A turn counter, or none at all. With actions taking time (sneaking is slower), a clock might matter.
