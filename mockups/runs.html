The run simulator

Runs generated from the world plan, to see the branching paths you asked for. Twenty levels, top to bottom; each level holds one to three maps side by side, and the lines are the ways down between them. A way can split, so the player chooses, and two ways can join again. Each map is the Over Dungeon or part of a themed area: a region, a region's small version (a sub-region, as you said), a transition, or another sub-region. The rules are listed under the run; the figures at the bottom come from a thousand runs.
