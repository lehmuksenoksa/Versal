The run simulator

Runs generated from the world plan, to see the branching paths you asked for. Twenty levels, top to bottom; each level holds one to three maps side by side, and the lines are the ways down between them. A way can split, so the player chooses, and two ways can join again. Each map is the Over Dungeon (the generic region the branching ways are made of) or part of a themed area: a region, a smaller region (one level: a region's smaller version, or the bandits' hold), a transition (a smaller region that connects), or a sub-region inside a region (the archive, the cult's sanctum). The rules are listed under the run; the figures at the bottom come from a thousand runs.
