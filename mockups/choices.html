The full game, round 4: a board of regions and goals

A brainstorm, not a proposal: 18 regions (R) and 7 reasons to go down (G). Mark each keep, maybe or drop, and mix, add or write your own in the comments. What they have to fit: about 10 levels in 3 or 4 regions, a boss guarding the way out of each; each level blends one kind of ground into the next; stealth in the dark, with the loop of light (oil, being seen) and dark (dread, hidden traps). So each region says how it bends that loop. Cost: exists (in the game), S (a variant), M (new things on existing ground), L (a new kind of ground; new looks mean mockup rounds). The second pass puts the kept ones together into 3 or 4 regions with the goal, with my critique and my picks. Write-up: spec/round4-regions.md.
